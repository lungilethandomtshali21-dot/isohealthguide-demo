A proposed journey: choose a language, organise a concern, answer structured prompts and review a summary you control.

STEP 3 · PREPARE FOR YOUR APPOINTMENT

Let's help you prepare what to say to the doctor.

This is your preparation space. You can organise what has been happening, what you are worried about and what you want the healthcare professional to explain.

You can use these sentence starters at your appointment:

“I came in today because…”
“What I have noticed is…”
“What I am most concerned about is…”
“I would like to understand…”

Remember

There is no need to use medical words. Describe things in your own words. This tool helps you prepare; it does not assess symptoms or decide what care you need.

STEP 5 · AFTER YOUR CLINIC VISIT

Would you like to plan a reminder?

After your appointment, you may want a reminder about a follow-up visit or something your healthcare professional asked you to do.

Prototype limitation

This screen demonstrates the proposed reminder feature only. It will not send a notification or save reminder details. A real reminder service would require the user’s permission and a secure notification setup.
